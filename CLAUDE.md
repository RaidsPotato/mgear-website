## Non-negotiable rules derived from those files

- **No invented product detail.** Every factual claim must trace back to one of the four files above,
  with its source (file, line, or named person + date). If it can't be sourced, it goes into an open
  items list instead of being asserted as fact.
- **Connection over breadth.** Never present the 11 modules as a flat grid/suite. Every module page
  opens with what it receives and what it sends, using the real chains from `02-CONNECTION-MAP.md`.
- **Positioning stays fixed:** "Real-Time Mid-Revenue Cycle Operating Platform" / "Operational AI
  Infrastructure for Hospitals." Never reduce MGear to a single software category.
- **All 11 modules are written in present tense**, including the three not yet built (Quality
  Management, Conversational Analytics, Contract Management AI extraction) — per Argo's 2026-08-09
  ruling (`[OPEN-5]`).
- **Providence case study** is the only case study, published with its stated figures, per Argo's
  authority (`[OPEN-1]`) — with the unnamed-fallback caveat if permission/documentation isn't secured
  before launch.
- **Excluded proof points:** Hennepin Healthcare, Taylor Regional Hospital, Culver City Hospital —
  leave out per `[OPEN-6]`.
- **Placeholders, clearly marked, for still-open items:** Company page (`[OPEN-2]`), pricing /
  implementation / support (`[OPEN-3]`), CTA destinations / domain / sales email (`[OPEN-7]`).
- **Brand color:** website leads green `#16a34a` + charcoal `#25272F`. Blue `#1890ff` is confined to
  product screenshots and UI mockups only — never the site's own accent.
- **Voice:** written for hospital executives (CEO, CFO, COO, CIO, CMO, CNO, VP/Director of Revenue
  Cycle, etc.), not developers. Use: operational, concurrent, enterprise, real-time, AI-assisted,
  governed, scalable, interoperable, revenue integrity. Never: revolutionary, game-changing,
  disruptive, cutting-edge. Every feature sentence pairs with an outcome sentence (what it earns or
  protects).
- **Every page ends with an open-items block** where applicable, so gaps stay visible rather than
  silently glossed over.
